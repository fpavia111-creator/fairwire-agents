# Chinook fixtures

Fictional IT contracts and payments (made-up vendors and owners), read as of 18 November 2026.

- `contracts.csv`: 6 contracts. Three auto-renewing contracts have notice deadlines on 30 November, 1 and 2 December; network monitoring also rises 9%; the service desk contract has no owner; a second endpoint protection contract overlaps the first, and its own notice date passed on 2 October; the telephony contract ended on 30 September.
- `spend.csv`: payments in 2026. The service desk tool was paid 18,500 against 15,000 a year; the old phone company was paid after its contract ended; Litware Cloud Storage has no contract.
- `settings.json`: 60 days look-ahead, 5% uplift cap.
