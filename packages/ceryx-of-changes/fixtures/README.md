# Ceryx fixtures

Fictional change calendar for November 2026.

- `changes.csv`: 8 changes. Two high-risk ERP changes overlap on 24 November; the new payment page is pending approval, has no test evidence, is high risk at 10:00 on a Friday and falls in the Black Friday freeze; an emergency firewall change has no back-out plan; a standard change needs no approval; the web shop had two failed changes.
- `freezes.csv`: the Black Friday freeze.
- `settings.json`: business hours 8 to 18; emergencies over 10% are flagged.
