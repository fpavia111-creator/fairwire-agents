# Aura fixtures

Fictional access points, networks, clients and rogues (made-up names and MAC addresses).

- `aps.csv`: five access points, two radios each. AP-HQ-02 is on 2.4 GHz channel 3, 80% busy and broadcasts six networks. AP-HQ-03 runs older firmware than the other MR46s. AP-WH-01 has been offline since 10 November.
- `ssids.csv`: a corporate network, an open guest network, a scanner network on WPA-TKIP, a camera network on WEP and a disabled open test network.
- `clients.csv`: three weak clients on AP-HQ-02 and three 5 GHz-capable clients stuck on 2.4 GHz on AP-HQ-01.
- `rogues.csv`: a rogue copying the Corp name, one plugged into the wired network, and a neighbour.
