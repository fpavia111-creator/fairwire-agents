- `lines.csv`: 8 lines over two months. Ben Kim uses 2 to 3 GB of a 50 GB plan; Cy Diaz and the reception landline have no use at all; Dee Lee ran up 267.50 in roaming; Eli Moss has a 9.99 premium charge; Ana Ortiz has two mobile lines; Fay Nunez left in September but her line is still billed.
- `leavers.csv`: one leaver.
- `settings.json`: roaming above 50 and two months without use are flagged.
