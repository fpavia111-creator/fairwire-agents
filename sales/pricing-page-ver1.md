# Fairwire pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your machine, map your exports (firewall, VPN, Wi-Fi, identity provider, device management, backup tool, service desk, cloud bill), set your thresholds, run the first review with your team and hand over the worklists. Prices follow the proposals Kurt approved on 18 November 2026 (network traffic and DNS $1,500, call quality $1,500, AI usage and retries $1,500, support $250 a month), refined for the full family of 31 agents.

## Support after setup

- $250 a month per package.
- $750 a month for the whole IT team.
- $2,000 a month for a partner rollout.

Support covers new export layouts when a vendor changes its columns, setting changes, and a monthly review call. It can be stopped any month; the agents keep working without it.

## What is and is not included

Included: installation on your machine, column mapping for your exports, settings (business hours, days to patch, retention policy, allowed countries, inside address ranges and the like), one training session, the first set of reports and drafts.

Not included: changing any live system (firewall, switch, controller, DNS, directory, device management, cloud account), sending notices to vendors, cancelling lines or contracts, legal, compliance or security-certification advice, penetration testing or incident response. Your exports stay on your machine; names, sign-in names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and client addresses are masked before anything reaches the AI model.

## Decisions written down

- The three proposed $1,500 packages keep their price and scope; the "all checks" proposal of $3,500 was made when the family had five agents, so the whole family of 31 is priced at $9,500 and the five-agent starter set is not sold separately.
- Group packages are priced between $2,000 and $3,000 by the number of agents and the number of exports to map (identity and devices take the most mapping).
- The partner rollout is the top of the $1,000 to $15,000 range set for every Fair Agents family.
